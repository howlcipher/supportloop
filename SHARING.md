# Sharing the current research milestone

The public repository and methods are suitable for a transparent early-project
demonstration. They are not a verified investigation of a support vendor or a
validated automated ticket classifier.

## Demonstrate what exists

Start with README.md, run the standard-library arithmetic demo, then read
docs/review_protocol.md and docs/engineering_findings.md. Show that a numeric
threshold can be met while the human gate remains pending. Explain that the
generated labels do not judge the underlying tickets.

The implemented contribution is reproducible review infrastructure: synthetic
histories, strict input/reference checks, provenance declarations and descriptive
agreement. Current software checks support these implementation claims. They do
not support resolution accuracy, reduced customer effort or a new market gap.

## Authorized observational case

The owner authorized naming subscription provider Entertainment and online-game in the
constructive case study on the project site/source repository. Share
https://howlcipher.github.io/supportloop/case-study.html or its public PDF. The
narrative is customer-reported through November 8, 2026, not independently
verified, and separate from synthetic performance evaluation. It publishes no
account identifiers or original private artifacts. No entitlement, SLA breach,
internal-review finding or validated improvement is asserted.

This permission does not authorize sending messages to subscription provider or posting on
LinkedIn. Other private drafts and original evidence remain local.

## Review other local drafts before posting

The coordinator keeps a LinkedIn article, preliminary case-study draft and
recipient technical brief in ignored publication_drafts/. These are AI-assisted
drafts for owner review. They are not uploaded, sent or asserted as the owner's
own writing. The public repo has no dependency on those files.

An anonymous methods article can discuss the public toolkit and its limitations.
A vendor-specific incident narrative needs redacted primary artifacts, a current
observation cutoff, exact quotations where used, and owner approval of the final
text. The current incident account is owner-reported. No ticket export or
historical checkout was inspected. A checkout-default hypothesis is not evidence
of manipulative intent or an incorrect support decision.

## Claims to verify before sharing

1. Link the exact commit and its passing CI rather than an old test result.
2. State the implemented scope and uncompleted human/benchmark gates.
3. Identify synthetic fixture statistics as arithmetic examples.
4. Cite primary product documentation for prior art without claiming measured
   adequacy or failure of those products.
5. Keep a justified denial distinct from an unfulfilled customer preference.
6. Confirm that public screenshots, exports and logs contain no private data.
7. Have the owner review the article and any recipient before sending anything.

No agent contacts a vendor, accesses private accounts or publishes incident
material as part of running this toolkit. A future completed case study may
honestly conclude that the proposed product should not be built.
