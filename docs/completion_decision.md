# Discovery completion decision

Decision date: November 8, 2026. Recommendation: **NARROW**.
This closes the current research preparation milestone, not the empirical study.

## Decision and rationale

Do not build a general ticket grader on current evidence. The broad feature
set overlaps established QA, while local portability and inspectable evidence
remain unvalidated opportunities. Complete the useful open methods artifact
and retain explicit conditions for further engineering. A stopping decision
is a legitimate research outcome.

The [prior-art inventory](prior_art.md) records documented capabilities and
its limits. No configured commercial comparison, customer interview or
purchasing evidence has established unmet demand. No product superiority is
claimed. The [evaluation contract](evaluation_contract.md) fixes the next gates;
this decision does not waive them.

## What can be shared now

An owner-reviewed methods article may describe the public code, reproducible
demo, prior art, engineering defects found and limits. It must say that human
review and accuracy evaluation have not happened. The article must not frame
synthetic agreement as successful ticket classification.

A vendor-specific narrative needs a separate evidence review. The initial
incident is an observational account, not a controlled experiment. Neither
an annual-plan default nor manipulative intent has been verified. A missing
visible response cannot establish missing internal investigation. An unfavorable
but justified decision need not be a support defect.

## Remaining gates and concrete inputs

| Gate | Required input | What proceeds after it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human feasibility | Two humans, independent of case authorship, with exposure declarations and complete original returns | Agreement and control-case review under the locked contract |
| Comparative discovery | Consented target workflow and access to an existing configured QA rubric | Decide whether configuration or an adapter is sufficient |
| Independent benchmark | New scenario families and independent labels before candidate development | Compare deterministic baselines before adding semantic models |
| Incident reconstruction | Redacted original reply, visible status/timestamps, observation cutoff and source provenance | Analyze what the artifacts actually support |
| Checkout hypothesis | Relevant historical initial/confirmation states, or a separately designed synthetic study | Assess defaults, clarity and correction opportunities without assuming intent |
| Publication | Owner review of exact final text and destination | Share only approved material |

No human reviewers are currently available. That is a dependency, not a
reason to synthesize labels, silently lower thresholds or build a classifier.
Recruitment and vendor contacts are not initiated by this project. Drafts and
an evidence-intake worksheet are prepared locally for the owner.

## Continuation rule

Use the roadmap when a missing input arrives. Reconcile disagreements before
claiming feasibility. If the existing rubric meets the workflow, stop product
expansion and keep the open methods or a small integration. If independent
review fails, revise or stop. Do not promise that further research will support
building a product.
